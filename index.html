About Us

Built on heat, pressure and precision.

Apex Rolling Mills is an integrated steel re-rolling facility converting billets into high-quality long products. Our modern mill combines a reheating furnace, a continuous rolling line and in-line quenching to deliver consistent mechanical properties in every bar.

From residential homes to bridges and power plants, our steel is trusted by contractors, engineers and dealers across the country.

  • Dedicated logistics and on-time dispatch

Manufacturing Process

From billet to bar

  1. 01

    Raw Material

    Carefully selected billets are chemically tested before entering the line.

  2. 02

    Reheating

    Billets are heated uniformly to ~1150 °C in a fuel-efficient reheating furnace.

  3. 03

    Rolling

    Roughing, intermediate and finishing stands reduce the section to its final profile.

  4. 04

    Quenching

    In-line thermo-mechanical treatment creates a hard outer case and ductile core.

  5. 05

    Cooling & Cutting

    Bars rest on the cooling bed, then are sheared to length and bundled.

  6. 06

    Testing & Dispatch

    Every lot is tested, tagged with heat numbers and dispatched with test certificates.

Quality Assurance

Tested before it leaves the mill.

Our NABL-standard laboratory checks every heat for chemistry and mechanical properties. Each bundle carries a heat number so every bar is traceable back to its billet.
