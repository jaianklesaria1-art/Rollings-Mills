why "rolling mills"?

A rolling mill reshapes steel. We're here to reshape beer.

Rolling Mills is an independent craft brewery in Mumbai, chasing expressive and provocative beers. A rolling mill changes the shape of metal. We want to change what people in India think craft beer can be.

Everything is brewed in our warehouse in Charkop Industrial Estate, Kandivali. Steel tanks, loud music, and a crew who'll happily argue about hops until closing time.
