about the taproom

Built by beer nerds.
Run by the crew.

Rolling Mills started in 2021 in a warehouse in Charkop Industrial Estate, Kandivali, with one obsession: brewing beers that make you stop mid-sip. What began as a brewhouse grew into collabs, a loyal crowd, and a taproom of our own.

Our Craft Beer Dispensary on New Link Road, Andheri West, is where it all comes together. Every pint is poured fresh from our tanks by the people who brewed it, with bar food built to go with beer and something happening most nights.

    why "rolling mills"?

    A rolling mill reshapes steel. We're here to reshape beer.

    Rolling Mills is an independent craft brewery in Mumbai, chasing expressive and provocative beers. A rolling mill changes the shape of metal. We want to change what India thinks craft beer can be.

    Every drop is brewed in our warehouse in Charkop Industrial Estate, Kandivali, then poured fresh at our taproom in Andheri West. We've even brewed a Mexican lager, Los Pablos, with our friends at Simba.
