    Our story

    It was always about the beer

    Rolling Mills started in 2021 in a warehouse in Charkop Industrial Estate, Kandivali, with one obsession: beer that makes you stop mid-sip. A rolling mill reshapes steel. We set out to reshape what Mumbai expects from craft beer.
