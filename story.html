Our story // est. 2021

Forged in the Mill

Before there was a taproom, there was a warehouse in Kandivali, a pile of steel tanks, and a crew who thought Mumbai deserved louder beer.

Day zero: 2020

Before the first pour

In November 2020, Rolling Mills Brewery was just a name on a company registration and an idea that wouldn't go away. The name came from the steel world: a rolling mill takes raw metal and reshapes it into something new. We wanted to do the same to what India thinks craft beer can be.

In 2021 the idea got a home: a warehouse in Charkop Industrial Estate, Kandivali. Tanks went in, the music went up, and the first batches started rolling out to Mumbai. No big launch, no big budget. Just good beer, made by people who drink it.
